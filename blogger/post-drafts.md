# Blogger 2026 editorial drafts (medical fact-check required)

## 요양병원 vs 요양원, 비용과 서비스 차이
Medical needs, legal facility classification, insurance coverage and room/care charges must be independently confirmed with each provider.

## 뇌경색 이후 재활요양병원 선택 가이드
Confirm functional assessments, therapy team, follow-up planning and inpatient acceptance criteria. Avoid promises of guaranteed recovery.

## 고주파 온열치료와 실손보험 체크리스트
Treatment efficacy, indications, billing codes and insurance claims differ case by case. Never promise reimbursement or replace standard oncology.

## 중증질환 산정특례와 요양병원 비용
Verify registered diagnosis, specific applicable claims, length of stay, patient class, food and non-covered costs via NHIS and actual institution.

## 투석환자 요양병원 입원 전 체크리스트
Confirm provider capacity, treatment schedule, infection-prevention protocols and emergency transfer arrangements.
